## H-002 observational-equivalence witness (proposition)

The previous falsifier was backwards. A procedure that, using only selected
rows, “establishes state-independent inclusion” on a table whose inclusion
*does* depend on \(X\) is an unsound false positive. It does not refute
impossibility.

**Witness** (binary \(X\), observed selected law \(Q=(1/2,1/2)\), known
overall inclusion rate \(2/3\)):

| Model | Source \(P\) | Selection \(\pi\) | Selected masses \(P(X=x,S=1)\) | Inclusion |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I (state-independent) | \((1/2,1/2)\) | \((2/3,2/3)\) | \((1/3,1/3)\) | \(2/3\) |
| II (state-dependent) | \((2/3,1/3)\) | \((1/2,1)\) | \((1/3,1/3)\) | \(2/3\) |

Selected rows plus the inclusion rate are identical. Rows alone cannot
distinguish the models without a source-law or selection assumption.

A genuine escape hatch is extra information that identifies \(P\) or \(\pi\):
a known source law, a randomized encouragement of inclusion, or an instrument
for selection. Declaring `state_independent_within_regime` is that assumption,
not a discovery. The software may *gate* on the declaration; it may not
*infer* it from the table.

Law 12: evidence that *selected* a pair (survey, parsimony frontier, DAG learner)
cannot also *test* it. Confirmation clusters or a new experiment only.

Law 13: an apparatus that cannot report “nothing” has not been shown capable of
reporting anything. Empty common support, `NO_PASS`, `MULTIPLE_PASSES`, and
`Underpowered` are load-bearing outputs.

Law 14: a check that cannot fail is not evidence. Running GCM on non-product
quotas is not “a test that always accepts.” The residual product can reject.
It is the **wrong null**: it tests design covariance, not density curvature.
The engine must refuse that track so the wrong null cannot be mistaken for
κ ≡ 0.

## Modes of reasoning (lead-agent synthesis, no NTM)

Selected because this is a **scientific audit system**, not a product CRUD app.
Axes that matter: ampliative vs non-ampliative; descriptive vs normative;
belief vs action.

| Mode | What it says about MIC |
|---|---|
| Deductive | The certificate is a biconditional. Software that “passes” without the three conditions is a type error. |
| Causal / deletion | Orientation is two-sample equivalence, not a residual heuristic. |
| Adversarial | The classic attack is swapping four-law and GCM. Keep the tracks unmixable. |
| Abductive | Leftover κ is a *search criterion* for missing measurements, not a DAG edge. |
| Worst-case | Selection, unit mismatch, and aliased partial designs fail closed. |
| Debiasing | Histogram flatness is not modularity. Survey ranking is not orientation. |
| Systems | Packet 1 FrankenPandas must implement `IngestReport`, not a parallel ingest. |

| Meta | Orchestration (mail, beads, “autonomous mode”) has no mathematical authority. |
