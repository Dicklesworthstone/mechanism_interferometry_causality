A causal-modularity calculus for factorial regimes

Outcome effects need not add.
Mechanism changes do.

Autonomous causal changes may interact arbitrarily in outcome space. In log-density-ratio space they add exactly. Every failure of that additivity is a gauge-invariant curvature field that separates nonlinear response from genuine mechanism coupling and from curvature manufactured by incomplete observation.

Four things one number cannot separate

An interaction coefficient is four answers wearing one coat.

Outcome-level interaction statistics conflate response nonlinearity, genuine coupling between mechanisms, curvature created by state you did not measure, and an intervention that was not implemented the way you specified. Mechanism interferometry moves the object of study from a chosen response summary to the full regime law, where the confusion is resolvable.

01 N

Response nonlinearity

The map from state to outcome can manufacture enormous synergy while the generating mechanisms stay perfectly autonomous.

02 C

Mechanism coupling

The combined intervention actually changes how a primitive mechanism operates. This is real density curvature, and the only case the word interaction should describe.

03 M

Missing state

Marginalizing away a variable makes exactly flat latent factors conditionally dependent. The curvature is a property of your observation set, not of the world.

04 I

Implementation drift

The joint arm was not the composition of the two single arms. A negative control with no interference must produce exactly zero curvature, or the apparatus is the finding.

The local likelihood-ratio hologram

re(x) = pe(x)p0(x) = qe(xt ∣ xpa(t))pt0(xt ∣ xpa(t))

For a soft intervention that replaces only the conditional mechanism of X_t, every unchanged factor cancels from the regime likelihood ratio. A perturbation can move every descendant marginal while its full joint ratio remains supported on the target and its direct parents alone. Localization is not an escape from conditional-independence testing; it relocates the search from state-to-state relations to design-to-state relations.

The publishable core

A finite biconditional certificate

Relative to a proposed graph and target assignment, three observable properties are necessary and sufficient for a modular soft-intervention representation of the whole regime family. Nothing else has to be checked.

Order two suffices

On a complete cube, vanishing square curvature makes every higher-order Möbius coefficient vanish automatically. Zero face curvature makes each edge increment invariant under flips of the other coordinates; the remaining cube is connected, so every edge increment equals its reference-corner value and the log-densities become exactly additive. Higher-order tests remain useful as diagnostics under estimation error, never as additional requirements.

Gauge invariance

Adding any field of the form a(x) + sum_j s_j b_j(x) to the log-densities leaves every mixed difference of order two or higher unchanged. The primitive potentials depend on the reference corner; the curvature does not. Reversing a coordinate orientation flips the sign of a mixed difference but never the statement that it is zero. A reference corner is a coordinate convenience, not a scientific baseline.

Curvature from hidden state

Three conservation laws

Whatever conditional coupling marginalization creates between flat latent factors must reappear, with opposite sign on average, as correlation between the observable primitive ratio fields. Curvature is not lost when you stop looking. It is moved.

Law 1 · curvature balance

Cov0(rA,rB) = −E0[rArB(eκ − 1)]

The cheap moment statistic is a signed, ratio-tilted average of curvature. Its blind spot is quantitative: a sign-varying field can integrate to zero.

Law 2 · latent conservation

Cov0(rA,rB) = −E0[Cov0(LA,LB ∣ X)]

Coupling hidden by observation returns with opposite sign as correlation between observable ratio fields. Neither side is directly the other; the average is exact.

Law 3 · nested state expansion

CX = E[CXW ∣ X] + Cov(rAXW,rBXW ∣ X)

Add a candidate measurement. If residual curvature disappears, the old curvature was variation of now-separated mechanisms across the new coordinate. Curvature becomes a search criterion.

Missing Fisher information

For infinitesimal perturbations, observed curvature is the conditional covariance of the complete-state intervention scores. That is exactly the off-diagonal missing-information term in Louis's decomposition, and therefore the negative of the corresponding observed-information cross entry when the complete cross-Hessian vanishes. State expansion recovers the missing mechanism information in stages by the tower property.

At orders three and above the clean two-term split no longer holds; Brillinger's conditioning formula requires set-partition sums. The tidy decomposition is special to covariance.

Flatness is never a reward

Curvature is preserved by any invertible transformation of the state, and by any learned representation that satisfies design sufficiency. That is what makes representation search legitimate. It is also what makes optimizing a representation towards flatness illegitimate: a model trained to minimize measured curvature has been trained to destroy the evidence. Representations are selected on held-out sufficiency and predictive composition, never on the flatness statistic itself.

Orientation without ratio estimation

Delete a coordinate. See what survives.

Within a localized family, delete each coordinate in turn. The target is the coordinate whose deletion leaves the remaining marginal law invariant. Estimation becomes a handful of low-dimensional two-sample equivalence tests rather than conditional means of a calibrated density ratio.

The master marginal identity

E0[re(X) ∣ XA] = pe(XA)p0(XA)

The identity is what makes deletion testing legitimate: the conditional mean of a high-dimensional ratio equals a low-dimensional marginal ratio, so the ratio never has to be estimated at all.

Inference without category mistakes

Two tracks, matched to the sampling design

Density curvature is a functional of four normalized regime laws. A residual-product test characterizes zero curvature only when the pooled design odds are one. Confusing the two does not weaken the test; it silently changes the null.

I

Four-law functionals

For arbitrary known, state-independent regime quotas.

Mw = E0[w(rAB − rArB)]

Estimable as E_AB[w] - E_A[w r_B] with ratio nuisances normalized on held-out baseline data. Needs overlap control, but no assignment model. Under positivity, vanishing for every bounded witness is equivalent to zero curvature almost surely.

II

Generalized covariance measure

For product factorial randomization, or explicit reweighting to it.

θw = E[w(A − mA)(B − mB)]

Cross-fitted residual products are Neyman-orthogonal. Weighted GCM supplies growing witness classes and max-statistic inference. No observation may both construct an adaptive witness and test it.

The sampling-odds anchor

κAB(x) = log q11(x)q00(x)q10(x)q01(x) − log ρ11ρ00ρ10ρ01

Curvature is excess conditional design log-odds after subtracting the known pooled-design odds. Equal-corner balancing is product sampling and is safe. Arbitrary per-corner quotas are not: under pooled odds rho, zero conditional covariance characterizes kappa = -log OR(rho), not kappa = 0. A free-intercept multinomial fit can hide the offending term entirely if the analyst reports a centered interaction instead of reconstructing the full posterior odds and subtracting.

A unified audit pipeline

Two statistical primitives, applied repeatedly

The whole protocol is built from weighted residual products and low-dimensional two-sample equivalence tests, over shared cross-fitting and cluster-resampling infrastructure.

  1. Validate design

    Sampling odds, selection contract, overlap, randomization unit.

  2. Localize

    Design-IAMB with cross-fitted residual-product independence tests.

  3. Orient

    Simultaneous MMD and energy-distance equivalence tests over deletions.

  4. Interfere

    Weighted GCM projections and joint multinomial interaction fields.

  5. Predict

    Self-normalized primitive-ratio products on held-out combinations.

  6. Expand state

    Find measurements that flatten reproducible curvature blocks.

Cross-fitting graph

Outer folds carry scientific evaluation and separate adaptive witnesses from their tests. Inner folds tune nuisances. The cluster bootstrap supplies uncertainty at the assignment unit, never at the row.

Compositional prediction

Held-out pair distributions are predicted by the product of primitive ratios. The raw normalizer residual is always reported alongside the self-normalized estimate, because self-normalization can hide the very failure being tested.

Longitudinal caution

Ratios are estimated at the transition level. Whole-trajectory ratio products are prohibited as a primary estimator: their variance grows exponentially in the horizon.

Partial designs are not a lost cause

When corners are missing, the testable content is the null space of the main-effects matrix. A three-factor cube missing only its two extreme corners contains no complete square face at all, yet six observations against rank four still leave two genuine flatness restrictions. An implementation that only enumerates squares would report, incorrectly, that nothing is testable.
